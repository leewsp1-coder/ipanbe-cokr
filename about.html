WHO WE ARE

ThePlanBe is a Seoul-based company delivering IT services, industrial solutions, technology convergence and trade — from planning to execution.

Founded in June 2015 and headquartered in Gangnam, Seoul, ThePlanBe Corporation works at the intersection of information technology, industrial engineering and international business. Our name says what we do: “The Plan B” is the execution plan — we turn our customers’ requirements into working systems, products and partnerships.

IT Services. We provide total IT services optimized for each customer’s industry and environment: smart control and surveillance platforms, construction project management information systems (PMIS), MRO (maintenance, repair and operation) systems, and digital transformation built on AI, big data and large language models.

Industrial Solutions. We develop and supply high-grade industrial technology and engineering services, including ultra-high-temperature combustion systems, composite gas cylinders, military defense goods such as IR-resistant paint and camouflage nets, and high-tech CNC milling machines.

Convergence. We create new value by combining advanced technologies: anti-drone and special-purpose drone systems with UTM, illegal radio-frequency detectors, mobile device management (MDM), and smart factory solutions based on digital twins.

Trade and Global Partnership. Through overseas direct investment expertise and our role in the Korea-Pakistan Business Association, we connect Korean technology with partners abroad, and our work extends into construction and infrastructure development, renewable energy, smart city platforms, system integration and business consulting.

Our mission is to enrich the future of our customers by improving their infrastructure and efficiency through industry-specific expertise — as a company that respects people and loves nature.
