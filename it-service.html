IT SERVICES

Smart Control System Platform (Surveillance)

An integrated monitoring and control platform that brings CCTV, sensors and access control into one operations screen. Events are detected and alarmed in real time so that operators can respond quickly across sites and facilities.

Construction PMIS

A project management information system for construction projects. Schedules, drawings, documents, costs and site reports are managed in one place and shared among the owner, contractors and supervisors.

MRO (Maintenance Repair and Operation)

Systems for maintenance, repair and operation of plants and equipment: asset registers, preventive maintenance plans, work orders, spare-parts inventory and history, so that downtime and operating cost go down.

Digital Transformation (AI/Bigdata/LLM)

Consulting and implementation of AI, big-data and large-language-model solutions: data platforms, analytics and prediction models, and LLM-based assistants that put a customer’s own documents and data to work.
