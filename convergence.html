CONVERGENCE

Anti-Drone, Special Drone, UTM

Detection and neutralization of unauthorized drones, special-purpose drones for inspection and defense, and UTM (unmanned traffic management) systems that control drone operations in a given airspace.

Illegal Radio Frequency Detector

Equipment and systems that detect, locate and record illegal or unauthorized radio-frequency transmissions, protecting facilities and communications from interference and eavesdropping.

MDM (Mobile Device Management)

Centralized management of corporate smartphones and tablets: device enrollment, security policies, app distribution and remote lock or wipe for lost devices.

Smart Factory Solution (Digital Twin)

A manufacturing optimization platform that collects sensor and equipment data (MQTT, Modbus, OPC-UA and more), stores it in a time-series database, and provides monitoring, quality and failure prediction on a digital twin of the plant.
