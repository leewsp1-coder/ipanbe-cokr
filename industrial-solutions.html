INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS

Ultra-High Temperature Combustion

Ultra-high-temperature combustion technology for industrial furnaces and waste treatment: complete combustion at high temperature, higher thermal efficiency and lower emissions.

Military Defense Goods (IR Paint/Cam Net)

Defense products that reduce detection by the naked eye, radar and thermal imaging: IR-resistant paint and multi-type camouflage nets (A, B and C types) for personnel, vehicles and facilities.

High Tech CNC Milling Machine

Supply and support of high-precision CNC milling machines for metal and composite parts, with tooling, programming and after-sales service for manufacturing customers.

Composite Cylinder

Composite-material gas cylinders that replace steel cylinders: about 30% lighter, corrosion-resistant, see-through for the remaining gas, and designed for a service life of over 30 years. We also provide plant design and engineering for composite cylinder production.
